Our story

Two brothers,
a garage,
and some Play-Doh

Pivot Point was founded by two brothers with decades of coaching experience at all levels of the game.

As former collegiate players, Brett and Kent Becker excel at breaking down the fundamentals to improve the way players approach hitting. After years of seeing players challenged with hand alignment on the bat, they created a product to improve a player's bat grip.

Pivot Point's mission is to help every player become a better hitter, and it starts with the proper grip. Whether you're a youth league beginner or a professional, softball or baseball player — every swing starts with a Pivot Point™.

The problem

Why gripping
the bat is
extremely hard

If you played or coached baseball or softball you have probably heard, “Don't grip the bat too tight, keep it loose in your hands.” Coaches would all agree that gripping the bat too tight is the easiest way to ruin a good swing. All of this is true — but most of us fail.

What if we told you the biggest offender of gripping the bat too tight is the skinny design of the bat handle?

Just like you, we were struggling to help players be consistent hitters, so we created a grip that would finally make the bat feel loose in their hands. Pivot Point Grips let you use your entire hand — not just your fingers — which puts you in a stronger, more comfortable position to create a faster hand pivot. The result: faster exit velocity and more consistent barrel accuracy.

The answer

How Pivot Point
Grips are
different

When we were developing our grip, we knew we had to make something that would feel bigger and be comfortable at the same time. The concept is simple: the greater the surface area your hands connect to the bat, the more control you have of the barrel.

We are delighted to have developed the first and only grip that improves player performance by just placing their hands on it.

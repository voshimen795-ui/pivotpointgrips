Sizing

Measure once,
then order

Measure from the bottom of your palm to the tip of your longest finger. Over 7.25″ takes the Adult grip. Anything smaller takes the Youth.

Both sizes carry the same three features — Power Grooves™ for hand alignment, ergonomic palm pads for full-hand contact, and an integrated knob for range of motion and barrel accuracy.

Not sure, or would rather not cut a knob off yourself? Email info@pivotpointgrips.com and we'll install it for you for free.

The problem

Why gripping
the bat is
extremely hard

If you played or coached baseball or softball you have probably heard, “Don't grip the bat too tight, keep it loose in your hands.” Coaches would all agree that gripping the bat too tight is the easiest way to ruin a good swing. All of this is true — but most of us fail.

What if we told you the biggest offender of gripping the bat too tight is the skinny design of the bat handle?

Just like you, we were struggling to help players be consistent hitters, so we created a grip that would finally make the bat feel loose in their hands. Pivot Point Grips let you use your entire hand — not just your fingers — which puts you in a stronger, more comfortable position to create a faster hand pivot. The result: faster exit velocity and more consistent barrel accuracy.

The concept is simple. The greater the surface area your hands connect to the bat, the more control you have of the barrel.

Grip installation

Six steps,
then 24 hours

Don't want to?
We'll do it free

What you need

  • Pivot Point Grip
  • Work bench or table vise
  • Hacksaw with metal blade
  • Box cutter
  • Safety glasses
  • Bucket
  • Shop rag
  • Solvent
  • Golf grip tape

  1. Remove the existing grip tape

    Carefully strip the handle back to bare.

  2. Remove the metal knob

    Use a hacksaw or metal blade saw (not included). DeMarini or hard plastic knobs: see the note below.

  3. Build up the handle with grip tape

    Adult: cut golf grip tape to 8.5″ and build the handle up to .92″–.93″. Youth: two pieces cut to 7.25″.

  4. Saturate the tape with solvent

    Work over a bucket and keep a shop rag close.

  5. Slide the grip on

    Align the palm pads with the brand label. You should feel resistance — if it slides on easily, go back to step 3 and add more tape.

  6. Let it sit for 24 hours

    Then take it to the cage.

DeMarini or hard plastic knob

Twist the knob until it breaks free — a vise helps. Then use a hammer and nail to remove the pin in the handle, and return to step 3.

Pivot Point was founded by two brothers with decades of coaching experience at all levels of the game. Which size do I need — Adult or Youth?

Adult size is recommended for hands larger than 7.25″, measured from the bottom of the palm to the longest finger. Anything smaller, go Youth.

What bats does the grip fit?

You can repurpose just about any bat into a Pivot Point Grips training bat. The existing knob comes off, the handle gets built up with grip tape, and the grip slides on.

I don't want to cut the knob off myself.

Then don't. If you're not comfortable removing the knob and installing the grip, we'll do it for you for free — email info@pivotpointgrips.com and we'll sort out the details.

What about DeMarini bats?

DeMarini bats are some of the best performing bats on the market, but the knobs break a lot — which makes them ideal candidates. Twist the knob until it breaks free, remove the pin in the handle with a hammer and nail, then follow the standard steps from step 3.

Do you do team orders?

Yes. Team pricing, custom bat colors and engraved team logos are all available — send us a note and we'll put a quote together.

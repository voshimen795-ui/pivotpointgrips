The difference

A traditional half bat

  • You set the hands yourself, rep after rep, and they move.
  • The hold drifts back to the one you already had.
  • A bad result could be the swing or could be the grip, and you cannot tell which.
  • So it gets used now and then, instead of every day.

The Finisher

  • The grip positions the bat. Your hands land in one place.
  • Power Grooves™ hold the alignment through the swing.
  • The hold is a constant, so the ball flight is about the swing.
  • It earns a place in the daily routine.

What does the positioning

Three things,
one piece

A half bat only teaches you something if your hands are in the right place. These are what put them there and keep them there.

About the grip

  • 01

    Power Grooves™

    Hand alignment that repeats itself, swing after swing. You cannot drift back into the old grip without noticing.

  • 02

    Ergonomic palm pads

    Contoured to the hands, so you grip with the whole hand rather than the fingers. More surface on the bat, more control of the barrel.

  • 03

    Integrated knob

    Built into the grip rather than bolted under it. Improves range of motion and barrel accuracy.

Youth or Adult — which do I need?

Measure the hitter’s hand from the bottom of the palm to the tip of the longest finger. Over 7.25″ takes the Adult grip; anything smaller takes the Youth. It is about hand size, not age.

What lengths does it come in?

More than one, and every length takes the same grip. Put the length you want in the order notes, or ask us and we will match it to the hitter.

Do I have to install the grip?

No. The Finisher ships with the Pivot Point Grip already fitted. Installing is only for putting a grip on a bat you already own — see the installation guide for that.

What can I hit with it?

JUGS Lite-Flite®, Marv Training Balls, Driveline Smash Factor Balls, wiffle balls, tennis balls and similar. Not hard baseballs or softballs — it is a training bat, and that is what the wood is built for. Tee, soft toss and machine work, indoors or out.

How long does a custom take?

Longer than a stock build — the barrel is finished in your colors and engraved. Get in touch if you have a date to hit.

Is there a discount for more than one?

Two or more training bats — Finisher, Iron and/or Sledge — take 10% off those lines, applied automatically in the cart. Teams get their own pricing — see team sales.

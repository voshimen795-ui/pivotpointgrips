Design system

Every token, component and motion rule the site is built from. Change a value here and it propagates — nothing is hard-coded twice.

01  /  Colour

--orange

#e68a18

Primary. Buttons, accents, headline highlight, CTA band.

--orange-hi

#ff9d2a

Hover lift on accent surfaces only.

--ember

#e25600

Deep accent. Links on cream, where orange is too light.

--gold

#f7c948

Logo highlight. Reserved.

--ink

#101010

Dark sections, body background, ink buttons.

--ink-raise

#1a1a1a

Cards and inputs on dark.

--ink-line

#2b2b2b

Hairlines and borders on dark.

--cream

#f2efe7

Light sections. Also the text colour on dark.

--cream-line

#d9d4c6

Hairlines on cream.

--paper

#fbfaf6

Cards on cream.

--on-dark-dim

#a3a099

Secondary text on dark.

--on-light-dim

#5d5a52

Secondary text on cream.

Orange is the only accent. It carries buttons, active states, numbers and the CTA band. Nothing else competes with it. On cream backgrounds use --ember for links — --orange does not hold contrast against --cream at body sizes.

02  /  Type

.display
Archivo 800

Every swing

.h2
Archivo 800

Section heading

.h3
Archivo 700

Card heading

.lede
Inter 400

Intro paragraph. Slightly larger than body, always capped at 46 characters so the measure stays readable.

body
Inter 400

Body copy at 17px with a 1.6 line height.

.eyebrow
Space Mono 400

Section label / 08 voices

Three families, three jobs. Archivo shouts, Inter explains, Space Mono labels. Display sizes are all clamp() — they scale with the viewport rather than stepping at breakpoints. Headlines carry -0.026em tracking; body carries none.

03  /  Buttons

Accent Ghost Ink

One accent button per view. .btn--ghost is the secondary on dark; .btn--ink and .btn--outline-ink are the pair used on the orange CTA band.

07  /  Motion

--ease

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) — the only curve on the site.

--dur-fast

0.2s — colour and border changes.

--dur-base

0.45s — transforms, hovers, card lifts.

--dur-slow

1.1s — scroll reveals and image wipes.

Every motion rule has a reduced-motion counterpart. The hero drift, pointer parallax, word stagger, image wipes and marquee all stop under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, and the layout is identical without them. Pointer effects are additionally gated behind (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) so touch devices never pay for them.

08  /  Image treatment

Photography wipes open from the bottom while the image settles out of a 1.14 over-scale, then takes a slow 1.05 push on hover. Captions rise 250ms behind the wipe. Every <img> carries width, height and loading="lazy" so nothing shifts as the page loads.

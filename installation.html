Grip installation

Six steps,
then 24 hours

Read the whole thing before you start, and wear the safety glasses. If any of it puts you off, we'll do it for free instead.

Have us install it

What you need

  • Pivot Point Grip
  • Work bench or table vise
  • Hacksaw with metal blade
  • Box cutter
  • Safety glasses
  • Bucket
  • Shop rag
  • Solvent
  • Golf grip tape

  1. Remove the existing grip tape

    Carefully strip the handle back to bare.

  2. Remove the metal knob

    Use a hacksaw or metal blade saw (not included). DeMarini or hard plastic knobs: see the note below.

  3. Build up the handle with grip tape

    Adult: cut golf grip tape to 8.5″ and build the handle up to .92″–.93″. Youth: two pieces cut to 7.25″.

  4. Saturate the tape with solvent

    Work over a bucket and keep a shop rag close.

  5. Slide the grip on

    Align the palm pads with the brand label. You should feel resistance — if it slides on easily, go back to step 3 and add more tape.

  6. Let it sit for 24 hours

    Then take it to the cage.

DeMarini or hard plastic knob

Twist the knob until it breaks free — a vise helps. Then use a hammer and nail to remove the pin in the handle, and return to step 3.

Before you buy

Assumption
of risk

By buying, installing or using a Pivot Point grip, you agree to follow the installation instructions provided with the grip, and not to modify the grip in any way.

Improper installation or modification can cause the product to malfunction. Risks include, but are not limited to, product damage and serious physical injury. Cutting a bat knob involves power tools and metal — wear the safety glasses, clamp the bat properly, and stop if anything feels wrong.

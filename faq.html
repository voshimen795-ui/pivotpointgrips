Ask us something

Which size do I need — Adult or Youth?

Adult size is recommended for hands larger than 7.25″, measured from the bottom of the palm to the longest finger. Anything smaller, go Youth.

What bats does the grip fit?

You can repurpose just about any bat into a Pivot Point Grips training bat. The existing knob comes off, the handle gets built up with grip tape, and the grip slides on.

I don't want to cut the knob off myself.

Then don't. If you're not comfortable removing the knob and installing the grip, we'll do it for you for free — email info@pivotpointgrips.com and we'll sort out the details.

What about DeMarini bats?

DeMarini bats are some of the best performing bats on the market, but the knobs break a lot — which makes them ideal candidates. Twist the knob until it breaks free, remove the pin in the handle with a hammer and nail, then follow the standard steps from step 3. The full sequence is on the installation page.

How long before I can use it?

Let the bat sit for 24 hours after installation. The solvent needs that long to set the tape under the grip.

Do you do team orders?

Yes. Team pricing, custom bat colors and engraved team logos are all available — see team sales or send us a note and we'll put a quote together.

Is there a discount on multiple bats?

Buy two or more training bats and take 10% off. Grips and training bats both carry mix & match pricing.
